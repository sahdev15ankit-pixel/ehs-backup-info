Privacy Policy

EHS Backup

Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. Introduction

EHS Backup is a private business backup utility being developed for EH Spaces & Design. It is intended to help protect business records, project documents and related information.

This policy explains how information is intended to be accessed, processed, stored and protected when the service becomes operational.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, the service may process:

  • Google account email address used to authenticate the user.
  • Files specifically created by or selected for the application.
  • File names, file identifiers, sizes and backup timestamps.
  • Business documents and project-related information included in backups.
  • Technical information required to operate and troubleshoot the service.

3. Google Drive Access

EHS Backup is designed to use Google's restricted drive.file permission.

This permission allows the application to access and manage only specific Google Drive files that are created by the application or explicitly made available to it by the user. It does not request unrestricted access to the user's entire Google Drive.

Google user data accessed through the application will be used only for the backup and restore functions authorised by the user. It will not be sold or used for advertising.

4. Purpose of Processing

Information may be processed to:

  • Create and maintain business data backups.
  • Restore authorised files when required.
  • Verify backup integrity.
  • Maintain application security and operational records.
  • Diagnose errors and improve reliability.

5. Storage and Security

The planned architecture uses encrypted backup files, restricted access controls and an owner-controlled encryption key.

The encryption design is intended to ensure that backup contents cannot be read without the corresponding private decryption key. The private key is intended to remain under the owner's control and is not intended to be uploaded to the application or backup storage providers.

These are planned security measures. They should not be interpreted as confirmation that every feature is already operational.

6. Third-Party Services

The service is designed to use infrastructure provided by third parties, including:

  • Google, for account authentication and authorised Drive storage.
  • Cloudflare, for application hosting and infrastructure.
  • GitHub, for source code hosting and deployment automation.

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

7. Sharing and Sale of Information

We do not sell personal information or Google user data. Information will not be shared with advertisers. Access by infrastructure providers is limited to what is necessary to operate the service.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Backup retention is intended to follow the configured backup retention policy. Users may request deletion or correction of information by contacting us.

Deletion from the application may not immediately remove copies retained by third-party providers or separate offline backups.
